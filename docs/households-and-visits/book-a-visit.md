# Book a visit

**Tab: Cases**

1. Open the household's case (see [Find a household](find-a-household.md)).
2. Scroll to **Book a visit**.
3. Choose the **Date** and **Time**.
4. **Type** is filled in for you (Assessment for a first visit).
5. Choose the **Adviser** who is visiting. If they are not listed, choose **Someone not on this list** and type their name.
6. Select **Check that day**. This shows what else is already booked that day so you can avoid a clash.
7. Add any **Notes** the adviser needs, such as access instructions.
8. Select **Book**.

The adviser appears in the diary and on the appointment letter. Advisers on the list also receive a calendar invitation by email. Somebody who is not on the list does not, so tell them yourself.

!!! tip "Good to know"
    The diary records what is booked. It does not know about leave, meetings or office closures, so it cannot stop a double booking. Always use **Check that day** first.
