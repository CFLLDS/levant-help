# End a case

**Tab: Cases, under Actions, "Withdraw, cancel or remove…"**

There are three different ways to end a case. Choosing the right one matters, because it decides what is kept on the record.

| Option | Use it when | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Withdraw consent** | The resident has given consent and now wants to stop, or someone else lives there now | The data service is told to stop sending readings and the withdrawal is recorded. **This cannot be undone.** To start again the resident would need to give a new consent |
| **Cancel the case** | The case was raised by mistake and no consent was ever given | The case is closed and its meters are stood down. No withdrawal is recorded, because none happened |
| **Remove data** | The case is already closed and the household's data should be removed | Some records are kept as funder evidence, some have identifying details stripped out, and most are deleted. **This cannot be undone.** Select **Show me what would be removed** first |

## Withdraw consent

1. Open the case and select **Withdraw, cancel or remove…**
2. Under **Why?** choose one of:
   - The resident asked us to stop
   - An adviser is stopping it on their behalf
   - Someone else lives there now
3. Under **In your own words**, say what happened, for example "Resident phoned and asked to stop".
4. Type **YES** in the confirmation box.
5. Select **Withdraw consent**.
