# Refer a job to the handyperson

**Tab: Handyperson**

1. On the **Cases** tab, open the household's case.
2. Under **Actions**, select **Handyperson referrals for this case**. The Handyperson tab opens with the case already chosen.
3. Choose the **Job**:
   - Draught-proof external door
   - Draught-proof windows (per room)
   - Letterbox brush or keyhole cover
   - Chimney balloon or draught excluder
   - Insulate and draught-seal loft hatch
   - Radiator reflector panels
   - Lag exposed hot-water pipework
   - Fit hot-water cylinder jacket
   - Hang thermal curtains or linings
   - Set heating controls and explain them
4. Add a **Note for the handyperson**: access, parking, anything about the resident they should know.
5. Select **Raise referral**.

Raise one referral per job. Referrals you have already raised for the case are listed underneath with their outcome. The outcome is reported back by the handyperson service. A completed job also appears on the case as a handyperson visit.
