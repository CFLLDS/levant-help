# Quick answers

**I cannot see a household I know exists.** It may belong to another partner, or a status button may be hiding it. Search by case reference. If it still does not appear, contact support.

**The numbers at the top look wrong.** They count your organisation's households only. Reload the page to refresh them.

**I booked a visit but the adviser did not get an invitation.** Invitations only go to advisers who are on the list. Anyone added by name needs telling directly.

**A household shows "not confirmed" under Data service.** The resident has signed but the data service has not confirmed. No readings will arrive until it does.

**I selected Reset on the New household form.** A box appears on the page asking **Start this check again?** Select **Clear the form** to start again, or **Keep what I have** to carry on where you were.

**I cannot see a closed case.** Withdrawn and cancelled cases are in the list. If a status button is selected, select **All**.

**I cannot see the My calls today, Outreach or Funnel tabs.** They only appear for people working on Energy Confidence Dorset outreach. Contact support if you should have them.
