# Households and visits

This section covers the **Households and visits** page, the place where most day-to-day housekeeping happens. You will use it to find a household, bring a new home on board, book and move visits, refer jobs to the handyperson, return phone calls and keep an eye on consent.

You reach it from the **Energy Confidence** link in the bar at the top of any page.

## Who this is for

Advisers, volunteers and coordinators at a delivery partner. You only see the households that belong to your organisation.

## The page at a glance

The page has three parts, from top to bottom.

**1. Four headline numbers.** These tell you what needs attention today.

| Number | What it means | What to do about it |
|---|---|---|
| Visits this week | Visits booked for this week | Open the Visit diary to see them |
| Cases to book | Households that have a case but no visit booked yet | Open the case and book a visit |
| Call backs waiting | People who rang the phone line and asked for a call | Open the Call backs tab and ring them |
| Consents not registered | The resident has signed, but the smart meter data service has not confirmed it, so no readings are arriving | Open Consent & evidence to see which homes |

**2. The Energy Confidence inbox.** A summary of the shared mailbox: how many messages are unread, how many arrived today, how old the oldest unread message is, and how many unread messages have attachments. If the oldest unread figure is more than a couple of days, somebody needs to clear the inbox.

**3. Six tabs.** Each one is a job you do.

| Tab | Use it to |
|---|---|
| [Cases](find-a-household.md) | Find a household, see where it stands, book a visit, end a case |
| [New household](new-household.md) | Check a home is eligible and bring it on board |
| [Visit diary](move-or-cancel-a-visit.md) | See everything that is booked, and move or cancel a visit |
| [Handyperson](handyperson-referral.md) | Ask the handyperson to do a job, and see what happened to it |
| [Call backs](call-backs.md) | Return calls from people who rang the phone line |
| [Consent & evidence](check-consent.md) | Check consent for every household and spot homes sending no data |
