# Ring today's households

**Tab: My calls today**

This tab is for Energy Confidence Dorset outreach. It lists the households you are due to ring today, most important first. Levant™ does not hold phone numbers: you ring from Charitylog, using the reference shown, and record what happened here.

## Who appears here

A household comes onto your list when:

- its letter went out a week ago and nobody has called yet (**Follow-up call due**),
- an earlier call went unanswered and the next try is due, or
- a visit fell through and needs rebooking.

A household does not appear while it is on a check (see [Greyed-out households](outreach.md#greyed-out-households)), after it has said no, or while its phone number is marked wrong.

## Make the call

1. Select **Copy ref** next to the Charitylog reference, and find the client in Charitylog.
2. Read any yellow notes on the card first. For example, **Letter came back: check the address on the call.**
3. Open **What to say** for the call script.
4. If there is a list headed **AI-suggested talking points — check before using**, treat it as ideas only. Check each point against what you know before you say it.
5. **Visit brief** opens a one-page summary of the home, useful if you book a visit.
6. Ring the client from Charitylog.

## Record what happened

Choose the **Call outcome**:

| Call outcome | What happens next |
|---|---|
| No answer | The next try is put on the list for the next working day. After three unanswered calls the household is closed as **Closed – unreachable** |
| Voicemail | The same as No answer |
| Book a visit | Fill in **Visit date and time** and **Visiting adviser**. The visit goes into the diary |
| Spoke – no visit needed | The call is recorded and no visit is booked |
| Declined | The household is not contacted again. It can be reopened if they change their mind |
| Opted out | The household is not contacted again about this service |
| Wrong phone number | The household leaves the call list until the number is put right in Charitylog |

Tick **Energy advice given on this call** if you gave advice on the phone. Only tick it when you did.

Select **Save**.

If the list says **No calls due today**, there is nobody to ring.
