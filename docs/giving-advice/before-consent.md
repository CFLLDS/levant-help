# A household before consent

For Energy Confidence Dorset outreach, you can open a household before the resident has given consent. There are no meter readings yet, so this view is smaller than a full advice file. It holds what public records say about the home, the local weather, and a place to record what you find on a visit.

## Opening it

1. Go to the [Outreach](../households-and-visits/outreach.md) tab on the **Cases** page.
2. Select the household's case reference in the **Case** column.

The page opens headed **Energy Confidence · before consent**, with the case reference and the address.

## The top of the page

- **"Came to us via:"** says how the household was referred.
- **Visit brief** opens a one-page brief in a new tab, to print and take on the visit.
- **"From public records — confirm on visit"** appears until someone has saved what they found at the home. Treat every detail as unconfirmed until then.

## Confirm the smart meter

This card only appears on cases of the kind **Needs review**. A smart meter was found at the building, but the portal could not tell which flat it belongs to.

1. Ask the resident for a recent electricity bill.
2. Enter the **Electricity MPAN**. It is the 13 digits on the bottom row of the box marked S on the bill.
3. If the home has gas, enter the **Gas MPRN** as well. This is optional.
4. Select **Check and confirm**.

The portal checks the meter number against the smart meter data service and the case's postcode. If it matches, the meter is confirmed and the page reloads. If it does not, nothing is changed and the message says why.

## The Characteristics tab

The same tab you use on a full advice file. See [Property details](property-details.md). It shows what public records hold about the building, the area, and the capture form.

You can also upload an energy bill here. Anything you save is kept against the case, and carries straight over to the household's advice file when the resident gives consent. You will not need to enter it twice.

## The Weather tab

| Card | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **The week ahead** | The outside temperature forecast for the postcode, day by day, with the low and the high. Cold nights are marked. A **Cold spell forecast** label means cold nights are on the way |
| **The last 13 months** | The average and coldest temperature for each month |

Use it to plan. If a cold spell is forecast, a visit or call before it arrives may matter more than one after.

!!! note "No readings, no welfare flags"
    Before consent, the portal cannot see how the home uses energy. It will not raise a welfare flag or a tariff saving for this household. Rely on what the resident tells you.
