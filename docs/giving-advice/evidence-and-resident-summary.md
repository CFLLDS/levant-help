# Evidence and the resident summary

**Tab: Evidence**

## Funder evidence pack

A summary of this household written for the funder. It is **export-safe**: the household appears by case reference only.

It sets out who was helped, the problem, the action, the evidence, the outcome, the measured energy saved, the caveats and how fresh the data is. It also states which figures are measured, which are modelled and which are scenarios.

1. Choose the **Quarter**. You can go back seven quarters.
2. Select **Funder PDF** for the export-safe version, or **Adviser PDF** for your own records.

Read the pack before you send it anywhere. If a statement does not match what you know about the household, do not send it; tell your coordinator.

## Comparison group

Compares households that have been advised with those that have not yet. It stays empty until enough advised households have a measured saving.

## Quarterly pack

The programme-wide summary for the quarter: homes with a saving, energy and carbon saved, tariff changes, and a plan, do, check, act commentary with commitments for the next quarter.

- The commentary is drafted automatically from the pack's own figures. **Review it before sending it to a funder.**
- While the quarter is still open, any export is marked **provisional**. Once finalised, figures and commentary are frozen.
- **Funder PDF** gives the document. **Machine-readable (JSON)** gives the data.

Most advisers will not need to produce the quarterly pack. Your coordinator does this.

## Resident summary

A friendly, plain-language summary for the resident, reached through a private link that expires. There is no address in the message.

1. Select **Review & approve**. You are shown exactly what the resident will see.
2. Read it as the resident would. Is it kind, clear and correct?
3. Approve it. Approving records your sign-off on that exact content. **Nothing is sent when you approve.**
4. Then either:
   - **Copy secret link** to paste into an email or letter, or
   - **Send by text** to text the link to the resident.

The portal will not let you copy or send until you have approved. If the figures change, review and approve again.

**Blocked on a red flag.** If the household has a red welfare flag you cannot review or send a summary. They need a phone call first.

Treat the link like the summary itself. Send it only to the resident, by the route they have agreed.
