# Time away

**Tab: Property & usage, "Time away"**

The portal notices stretches of several days when the meter saw almost nothing happening. It lists them with the dates and the number of days.

The meter cannot tell a holiday from a hospital stay from a home that has been emptied. That takes somebody who knows the household, which is you.

## Review an absence

For each stretch marked as needing a decision:

1. Ask the resident, or check what you already know.
2. If they were away, choose the reason: **Holiday / away**, **Working away**, **Hospital or care**, **Moved out** or **Something else**, then select **Yes, away**.
3. If they were at home, select **No**.

Confirmed entries show the reason and who confirmed it.

## Why it matters

- **Savings stay honest.** Confirmed days away are taken out of the household's measured savings, so a saving reflects advice and not an empty house. Until the days are reviewed, any saving is marked provisional.
- **"No" is important too.** If someone was at home and the meter saw almost nothing, that may mean the heating and cooker were off. Treat it as a reason for a wellbeing conversation.
- **Moved out** should also be reported to your coordinator, because the consent belongs to the person who has left. See [End a case](../households-and-visits/end-a-case.md).

If a household has a long list of short stretches, start with the most recent and the longest.
