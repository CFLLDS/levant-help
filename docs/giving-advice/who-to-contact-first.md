# Who to contact first

Open your portfolio with **Sort** set to **Severity**. Then work down in this order.

## 1. Red welfare flags

A red flag means **ring them today**. The portal will say "Place a wellbeing call today and log the outcome. Energy actions can wait."

- Use **Contact** to phone. A text is not enough, and the portal will not let you send one.
- The call is about how the person is. Leave tariffs and bills for another day.
- [Record the call](record-what-happened.md) as soon as you put the phone down.

## 2. Welfare flags that are past the target

The line under the portfolio totals names any household whose flag has been open longer than the response target with no call or visit logged. These come next, oldest first.

## 3. Other welfare flags

Read the sentence on the card. Typical reasons are:

- the daily routine has changed and no longer matches the home's usual pattern
- electricity use is well above or well below what is normal for this home
- it has been quieter than usual for two days running

None of these means something is wrong. They mean it is worth a friendly call.

Before you ring, open the advice file and check two things:

- **Is data still arriving?** If the file says no readings are arriving, the flag shows the last known state. Say so on the call, and check the supply.
- **Has someone confirmed they are away?** See [Time away](time-away.md).

## 4. High usage

These households are using a lot more than similar homes nearby, or more than they used to. It can point to electric heating, a faulty appliance, or simply a full house. Approach it as a supportive conversation, never as a complaint about their usage.

## 5. Settled households

Nothing needs you today. Use quieter time to work through settled households that have not had a call or visit this quarter.

!!! tip "When the meter cannot watch a home"
    Some advice files say the meter cannot watch the household. This is common where solar panels or a battery cover the home during the day. Nothing is wrong with the home, but the portal cannot raise a reliable welfare flag for it. Rely on regular contact for those households.
