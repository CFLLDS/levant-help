## The portfolios page

When you open the portal you see one card for each organisation whose households are in your care. Each card shows the energy used on the last full day, that day's spend, and the number of households.

Select **Open portfolio** to see the households.

## The portfolio page

**Across the top** are four totals for the whole portfolio: households, spend, energy and carbon over the last 12 months. Some homes have less than a year of data, so the totals add up different lengths of time and will be on the low side. Select the small **i** beside a figure to read how it is worked out.

**Lines under the totals** may appear above the cards:

- **"… welfare flags past the response target"** names households whose flag has been open longer than the response target with nobody calling or visiting. Deal with these first.
- **"… households with a stale feed"** names households where no readings are arriving. Any welfare reading for them is the last known state, not today's. **Check the supply before calling.**
- **"Cold weather is forecast"** means cold-weather texts are ready to send. See [Cold-weather texts](#cold-weather-texts) below.

**Filters.** The buttons show how many households are in each group. A button only appears when at least one household is in that group, so you will not always see all of them:

- **All**
- **Welfare**: something in the pattern of use suggests checking the person is all right
- **High usage**: using noticeably more than similar homes nearby, or more than usual
- **Tariff**: a cheaper tariff looks worth a review, a fixed deal is ending, or the tariff has not been confirmed
- **Needs data**: readings have stopped or are running behind, or the household has only just joined
- **Settled**: nothing needs you today

**Search** finds a household by name. **Sort** reorders the cards by severity, spend, energy or name. Severity is the default and puts the households that most need you at the top.

## Reading a household card

| Part of the card | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| Status at the top | How the home is doing. See the table below |
| Sentence under the name | Why it has that status, in plain words |
| "Opened today", "open 3 days" | How long the flag has been waiting. "Past the target" means it has waited too long |
| "· picked up by …" | Someone has made contact and the response clock has stopped. The flag stays until the home's usual pattern returns |
| Annual spend and annual energy | The last 12 months, electricity and gas together. "Net annual cost" appears where the home sells electricity back to the grid |
| "Spend so far", "Part year — 7 months of data, not a full 12" | The home has less than 12 months of data. The figure is what has been recorded, not a yearly estimate |
| Data line at the bottom | The dates the readings cover, and how many months hold data |

The status at the top reads as follows. Where more than one applies, the card shows the most serious.

| Shown as | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **No data for 12 days** | No readings have arrived for 10 days or more | Check the supply. Any welfare finding underneath is the last known state |
| **Welfare — worth a call today** | A red welfare flag | Ring today. See [Who to contact first](who-to-contact-first.md) |
| **Welfare — worth a check** | An amber welfare flag | A friendly call soon |
| **Meter behind** | Readings are a few days late | Usually catches up by itself. Keep an eye on it |
| **High usage — worth a check** | Using more than similar homes nearby | A supportive conversation about what is running |
| **Tariff worth reviewing** | A cheaper tariff looks available | Look at the Call tab before you raise it |
| **Onboarding** | The household has just joined and its readings are still loading | Nothing yet |
| **Settled** | No concerns from the latest data | Nothing today |

Select a card to open the household's [advice file](the-advice-file.md).

Select **All portfolios** at the top left to go back to your list of portfolios.

## Cold-weather texts

When cold weather is forecast, a panel appears under the totals: **Cold weather is forecast.** It says why, and how many texts are ready and how many were skipped.

1. Select **Review the texts** to read the message and see each household on the list.
2. Select **Send N texts** to send them all, or **Dismiss** if they are not needed.

Only residents who have agreed to receive texts get one. A household is skipped if no permission to text has been recorded, or if it has a red welfare flag. Phone those households in place of a text.

If a resident has told you they are happy to receive texts, select **Resident agreed to texts** beside their entry and confirm. Permission is never assumed, so only select it when the resident has said yes.

Once sent, the panel reads **Cold-weather texts sent.**, with how many, when and by whom.

## If something is missing

- **"No households are being monitored yet"** means nobody in this portfolio has given consent and started sending readings. Cases still in outreach, onboarding or waiting for a visit are on the **Cases** page.
- **"No customers are visible for your sign-in yet"** means your account has not been given a portfolio. Contact your programme coordinator.
- **"You don't have access to this"** is a limit on your account, not a sign-in problem. Signing in again will not change it. Ask your coordinator to add the household to your access.
