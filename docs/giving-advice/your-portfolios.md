## The portfolios page

When you open the portal you see one card for each organisation whose households are in your care. Each card shows the energy used on the last full day, that day's spend, and the number of households.

Select **Open portfolio** to see the households.

## The portfolio page

**Across the top** are four totals for the whole portfolio: households, spend, energy and carbon over the last 12 months. Some homes have less than a year of data, so the totals add up different lengths of time and will be on the low side. Select the small **i** beside a figure to read how it is worked out.

**A line under the totals** appears when a welfare flag has been open longer than the response target with nobody calling or visiting. It names the household. Deal with these first.

**Filters.** The buttons show how many households are in each group:

- **All**
- **Welfare**: something in the pattern of use suggests checking the person is all right
- **High usage**: using noticeably more than similar homes nearby, or more than usual
- **Settled**: nothing needs you today

**Search** finds a household by name. **Sort** reorders the cards by severity, spend, energy or name. Severity is the default and puts the households that most need you at the top.

## Reading a household card

| Part of the card | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| Status at the top | Welfare, High usage or Settled |
| Sentence under the name | Why it has that status, in plain words |
| "Opened today", "open 3 days" | How long the flag has been waiting. "Past the target" means it has waited too long |
| Annual spend and annual energy | The last 12 months, electricity and gas together. "Net annual cost" appears where the home sells electricity back to the grid |
| "Spend so far", "Part year" | The home has less than 12 months of data. The figure is what has been recorded, not a yearly estimate |
| Data line at the bottom | The dates the readings cover, and how many months hold data |

Select a card to open the household's [advice file](the-advice-file.md).

## If something is missing

- **"No customers are visible for your sign-in yet"** means your account has not been given a portfolio. Contact your programme coordinator.
- **"You don't have access to this"** is a limit on your account, not a sign-in problem. Signing in again will not change it. Ask your coordinator to add the household to your access.
