# Record what happened

**Tab: Interactions**

If it is not recorded, it did not happen as far as the programme and its funder are concerned. Record every contact on the day.

## This quarter

Tiles across the top count this quarter's **visits**, **calls**, **check-ins**, **consents** and **letters**, with a total.

- Calls made with **Contact** and texts sent with **Check-in** are counted automatically.
- Consents are counted when the resident signs during onboarding.
- Everything else is counted when you log it below.

## Log a visit, call, note or letter

1. Scroll to **Log a visit or note**.
2. Choose the type: **Visit**, **Call**, **Note** or **Letter**.
3. Write what happened. Stick to facts: what was discussed, what was agreed, what you will do next. Do not record health details or opinions about the person.
4. Check the **Counts toward the funder rollup** box.
   - Visits and calls are ticked for you, because they are the funded advice activity.
   - Notes and letters are not. Tick the box only if this one was energy advice.
5. Select **Log it**.

The log is **append-only**. You cannot edit or delete an entry. If you make a mistake, add a new note that corrects it.

## The start and end survey

The funder measures outcomes by asking the same four questions twice: once at the start (**Baseline**) and once at the end (**Exit**).

1. Choose **Baseline (start)** or **Exit (end)**.
2. Choose **Resident agreed to answer** or **Resident declined**.
3. Ask the four questions and choose the answer given:
   - Is the home warm enough in winter?
   - How manageable are the energy bills?
   - Has heating been rationed or gone off to save money?
   - Confidence in managing energy use and costs
4. Select **Record survey**.

Good to know:

- **"Prefer not to say" is an answer.** Record it. Do not leave the question blank.
- You must answer at least one question, or choose "Resident declined".
- **Only a pair counts.** A baseline on its own is work in progress. The funder counts an outcome when both a baseline and an exit are recorded, so plan the exit survey when you record the baseline.
- Ask the questions as written. An exit that asks something different cannot be compared with its baseline.
