# Prepare for a call or visit

**Tab: Call**

The Call tab is written to be read in two minutes before you pick up the phone. Read it from top to bottom.

## 1. The summary

A short paragraph describing the home: how it is heated, where the money goes, and whether the welfare signals are calm.

## 2. Measured energy saved

Shown once the household has joined the programme. It compares their use since joining with what would be expected for the weather.

| You see | It means |
|---|---|
| **Baseline forming**, "too early to measure" | Not enough time has passed. Nothing to report yet |
| A saving marked significant | Use has dropped by more than chance would explain |
| **Lower usage, wellbeing check** | Use has dropped **and** there is a welfare flag. Treat the drop as a reason to check in, not as a success. It is only counted as a saving after a wellbeing call confirms the person is warm and well |
| "Absence review outstanding" | Some quiet days have not been explained yet. See [Time away](time-away.md) |

**Backup for the call** lists what the meter shows, such as when a change started. **Worth raising on the call** lists possible topics such as a heat pump or solar. These are scenarios, not recommendations.

## 3. Things to say on the call

Up to five prompts, in priority order. Follow them. In particular:

- **"Do not offer a tariff switch on this data"** means exactly that. There is not enough evidence to name a saving.
- If it says the priority is a wellbeing call, start and finish there.

## 4. What you can offer

| Shown as | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Already best** | The current tariff is the cheapest of those modelled. Nothing to switch |
| **Switch & save** | A cheaper option was found, with the modelled saving. Use your judgement on small amounts: a few pounds a year is not worth a resident's effort or the risk of a worse deal later |
| **Tariff**, "no tariff switch to offer" | The portal has ruled a switch out and says why. Usually there is too little data to name a saving, or a time-of-use tariff would only pay if the household could move its use away from the evening |

Tariff figures are modelled and indicative. You can explain the options. The choice of supplier and tariff is the resident's.

## 5. Keep an eye on

A watch list, for example a change in how often the home appears occupied, a rise in overnight use, or a note that the best fixed deal would cost more than the current rate.

## 6. Worth a welfare check

Appears when electricity use is well above the typical home in the same postcode. It gives this home's yearly figure, the typical figure, and how many nearby homes the comparison uses.

## 7. Next best action

One sentence, with **Send check-in** and **Contact via Teams** underneath.

## A two-minute checklist

- [ ] Status and reason read
- [ ] Resident's preferred contact route checked on their case
- [ ] "Things to say" read, and any "do not" noted
- [ ] Last contact checked on the Interactions tab
- [ ] For a visit, the Call tab printed with **Print / PDF**
