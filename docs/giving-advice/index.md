# Giving advice with the portal

The Levant portal tells you who to talk to, what to talk about, and what the meter shows, before the household has to ask. This section takes you through a working session from start to finish.

You reach it from the **Portal** link in the bar at the top of any page.

## The shape of a session

| Step | What you do | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open your portfolio and see who needs you | [Your portfolios](your-portfolios.md) |
| 2 | Decide who to contact first | [Who to contact first](who-to-contact-first.md) |
| 3 | Open the household's advice file | [The advice file](the-advice-file.md) |
| 4 | Get ready for the conversation | [Prepare for a call or visit](prepare-for-a-call.md) |

## How far to trust a figure

Every card in an advice file carries a label. Read it before you repeat a number to a resident.

| Label | What it means | How to use it |
|---|---|---|
| **Measured** | Comes straight from the home's smart meter or a sensor | You can quote it |
| **Modelled** | Worked out from measured data and published rates | Indicative. Say "about" and never present it as a quote or a bill |
| **Scenario** | Describes a change the home has not made, such as solar panels or a heat pump | A conversation starter only. Point the resident to a specialist before any decision |

Three more habits that keep advice safe:

- **Check how much data there is.** A home with a few months of readings shows "so far" figures. Do not turn them into a yearly figure yourself.
- **The person comes first.** If the portal flags a welfare concern, the conversation is about how they are, not about the bill.
- **If a figure looks wrong, do not quote it.** Note it, carry on with the conversation, and tell your programme coordinator afterwards.
