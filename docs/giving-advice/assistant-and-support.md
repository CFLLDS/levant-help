## The adviser assistant

The **Assistant** button sits at the bottom right of every portal page.

- On a portfolio page it answers across all your households, for example "Which households need attention this week?"
- Inside an advice file it answers about that household, for example "What can I tell this customer on this call?" or "Summarise what changed since the last contact."

Select one of the suggested questions or type your own, then select **Ask**.

Three things to know:

- **It assists only.** It never contacts residents and never changes a record.
- **Every question is logged to your name.** Ask it the way you would ask a colleague, and do not type in anything about a resident that is not already on their file.
- **You decide.** It can be wrong. Check what it says against the advice file before you act on it or repeat it.

### Reading an answer

- **Sources** under an answer lists up to four places it drew on. A help centre page is a link you can open. A reference to your Level 3 manual is shown as a citation without a link, so look it up in your own copy.
- **"Sovereign AI · answered by LDS's own on-premise model, in the UK"** appears under an answer written by LDS's own model, hosted in the UK. It only appears when that is true of the answer in front of you.
- **Withheld** at the top of a reply means the assistant chose not to answer. For example, it will not draft anything for a resident with a red welfare flag, because that household needs a call.
- **Not answered** means something went wrong, or the question took too long. Select **Try again**. Questions about one household are quicker than questions across the whole portfolio.
- If an answer ends by saying some data could not be read, it may be incomplete. Check the advice file.
- **Daily limit reached** in the question box means you have used today's allowance of questions. The box opens again tomorrow. Use the advice file and this help centre in the meantime.

## Help and support

Select **Support** at the top right of a portfolio page. A help page opens and a chat window appears at the bottom right of your screen. You can ask how to use the portal, how to read an advice file, or what a figure means.

The Support page also has:

- **Guides for every screen**: links to this help centre, including **Giving advice**, **Households and visits** and the **Course map**.
- **The energy awareness manual**: a reminder of which elements of your Level 3 manual cover tariffs and fuel costs, heating controls, help with measures, and damp and cold homes.
- **Prefer a person?**: a reminder that you can always contact your usual contact directly.

If the chat does not load, or you would rather talk to a person, contact your programme coordinator. The assistant is there to save you a step, not to replace them.

## Common messages

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Working out this home's figures" | Wait. It can take up to 30 seconds |
| "You don't have access to this" | Ask your coordinator to add the household to your access. Signing in again will not help |
| "These figures were last recalculated on..." | The overnight update has not run. Follow the wording in the banner |
| "Check-in texting isn't switched on yet" | Texting has not been set up for your organisation. Phone in its place |
| "Blocked: a red welfare flag needs a call" | Use **Contact** and ring the household |
