# Video scripts: Households and visits

*For HeyGen. Draft 0.1*

## How to use this file

Each clip below is a separate short video, so it can sit on the matching help page. For each one:

1. Record the screen following the **Shot list**, using a test household only. Record at 1920 x 1080, browser zoom 110%, with the DEV badge hidden.
2. In HeyGen, create a new video, choose the avatar and a UK English voice, and paste the **Narration** into the script box one scene at a time.
3. Put the avatar full screen for the first and last scene, and as a small circle bottom right over the screen recording for the scenes in between.
4. Narration is written at about 140 words a minute. Timings are a guide.

Pronunciation notes for the voice: "Levant" as leh-VANT. Read "EW-CASE" as "E W case". Read "n3rgy" as "energy".

## Clip 1: A tour of Households and visits (about 1 min 45 s)

| Scene | On screen | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Avatar full screen | Welcome to Energy Confidence. In the next couple of minutes I'll show you the Households and visits page. It's where you'll do most of your day-to-day housekeeping. |
| 2 | Click **Energy Confidence** in the top bar. Page loads | You'll find it under Energy Confidence, in the bar at the top of every page. |
| 3 | Slow highlight across the four headline numbers | Start with the four numbers at the top. Visits this week. Cases that still need a visit booking. People waiting for a call back. And consents that have been signed, but aren't yet sending data. If any of these isn't zero, that's your to-do list. |
| 4 | Highlight the inbox panel | Below that is the shared inbox. Keep an eye on the oldest unread figure. If it's creeping up, somebody needs to clear the mailbox. |
| 5 | Click each tab in turn, pausing one second on each | Then six tabs, one for each job. Cases, to find a household and book a visit. New household, to bring a home on board. The Visit diary. Handyperson referrals. Call backs. And Consent and evidence. |
| 6 | Avatar full screen | Each of those has its own short video. Pick the one you need, and you'll be up and running in a minute or two. |

## Clip 2: Find a household and book a visit (about 1 min 30 s)

| Scene | On screen | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cases tab. Type a postcode in Search, click **Search** | To find a household, search by name, postcode or case reference. |
| 2 | Click **Live**, then **All** | The buttons underneath filter the list. Live means readings are arriving. Consent given means the resident has signed, but the home isn't live yet. |
| 3 | Click the test household. Case opens on the right. Slow scroll | Select the household and its case opens on the right. You'll see how they like to be contacted, their visits, and their consent, one line for each meter. |
| 4 | Scroll to **Book a visit**. Fill in date and time. Choose an adviser | To book, choose the date, the time, and who's visiting. |
| 5 | Click **Check that day**. Result appears | Before you book, always select Check that day. The diary doesn't know about leave or meetings, so this is how you avoid a clash. |
| 6 | Add a note. Click **Book** | Add any notes the adviser needs, then select Book. Advisers on the list get a calendar invitation automatically. |

## Clip 3: Move or cancel a visit (about 1 min)

| Scene | On screen | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Click **Visit diary**. Change Days, click **Show** | The Visit diary shows everything that's booked. Set the dates you want and select Show. |
| 2 | Click **Week**, then **Month**, then an adviser's name button | You can switch between a list, a week and a month, and show one adviser at a time. |
| 3 | Back to **List**. Click **Move** on the test visit. Dialog opens | To rearrange a visit, select Move. Pick the new date and time, and say why. The reason is saved on the record. |
| 4 | Click **Keep as it is**. Then click **Cancel** on the same visit | Cancel works the same way. Give a reason, then confirm. |
| 5 | Click **Keep as it is** | And if you've opened one of these by mistake, Keep as it is closes it without changing anything. |

## Clip 4: Bring a new household on board (about 2 min)

*Hold until steps 3 to 8 have been walked through with a test household.*

| Scene | On screen | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Click **New household**. Highlight the eight-step progress line | Bringing a new home on board is a guided journey. The line across the top shows where you are. |
| 2 | Type a postcode, click **Find addresses**, pick an address | Start with the postcode, then pick the address from the list. Always pick it, don't type it. That's how the system tells a wrong address from a meter it can't see. |
| 3 | Step 2 appears, pre-filled. Click **Check eligibility** | The address is filled in for you. Select Check eligibility, and we ask the smart meter data service whether it can see a meter at this home. |
| 4 onwards | To be written | To be written |

## Clip 5: Refer a job to the handyperson (about 50 s)

| Scene | On screen | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open the test case. Scroll to Actions. Click **Handyperson referrals for this case** | If a home needs a practical job doing, open the case and select Handyperson referrals. |
| 2 | Open the **Job** list, scroll slowly through it | Choose the job. Draught-proofing, radiator panels, pipe lagging, a cylinder jacket, thermal curtains, or setting up the heating controls. |
| 3 | Type a note. Click **Raise referral** | Add a note about access or anything the handyperson should know, and raise the referral. |
| 4 | Referral appears in the list below | It appears underneath, and the outcome is filled in when the job's been done. |

## Clip 6: Return a call back (about 45 s)

| Scene | On screen | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Click **Call backs** | When someone rings the phone line and asks for a call, they appear here. |
| 2 | Highlight a row | You'll see who they are, how to reach them, and what it was about. Give them a ring. |
| 3 | Hover **Mark done**, then **Link to a case** | Afterwards, either mark it done, or, if they already have a case, link it, so the conversation is on their record. |

## Clip 7: Ending a case the right way (about 1 min 15 s)

| Scene | On screen | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Avatar full screen | There are three ways to end a case, and choosing the right one matters, because it decides what stays on the record. |
| 2 | Open the test case. Click **Withdraw, cancel or remove…** | Open the case, and under Actions select Withdraw, cancel or remove. |
| 3 | Highlight the Withdraw consent panel | If the resident has given consent and wants to stop, use Withdraw consent. This tells the data service to stop sending readings. It can't be undone. |
| 4 | Show the **Why?** list, the free text box and the YES box | Choose why, say what happened in your own words, and type YES to confirm. |
| 5 | Avatar, small | If the case was simply raised by mistake and nobody ever consented, cancel the case instead. And once a case is closed, Remove data clears the household's details. Always select Show me what would be removed first. |
